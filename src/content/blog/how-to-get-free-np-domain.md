## What is a .np Domain?

A **.np domain** is Nepal’s official country code top-level domain (ccTLD).  
It is provided **free of cost** to Nepali citizens and registered organizations by the official registry.

You can register domains such as:
- `.com.np` – for personal and business use
- `.org.np` – for organizations
- `.net.np` – for network-related services
- `.edu.np` – for educational institutions

Unlike `.com` or other international domains, **.np domains do not require annual renewal fees**.

---

## Who Can Apply for a Free .np Domain?

You can apply for a free .np domain if you are:

- A **Nepali citizen**
- A **registered company, organization, or institution** in Nepal

The **documents and rules differ** depending on whether the domain is:
- Personal (individual)
- Company / organization related

---

## Official Website to Register .np Domain

All .np domains are registered through the **official registry website**:

👉 https://register.com.np/

This platform is operated by **Mercantile Communications**, the authorized administrator of .np domains.

⚠️ You should **only** use this website. No third-party registrar can issue .np domains.

---

## Step 1: Create an Account on register.com.np

1. Visit https://register.com.np/
2. Click on **Register** or **Create Account**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name (must match your citizenship or company documents)
   - Email address (use a valid and accessible email)
   - Username and password
4. Verify your email address

✅ Tip:
- Use your **real name** and **real email**. Fake or mismatched details often lead to rejection later.

---

## Step 2: Search for Domain Availability

After logging in:

1. Go to the **Domain Registration** section
2. Enter your desired domain name (example: `yourname.com.np`)
3. Click **Search**

If the domain is available, you can proceed with the application.

⚠️ Important Rules:
- The domain name **must closely match**:
  - Your personal name, OR
  - Your company / brand name
- Random or unrelated domain names are usually rejected

Example:
- ✅ `sunleafsystem.com.np` → OK for Sunleaf System
- ❌ `besthostingnepal.com.np` → Likely rejected if no matching business

---

## Step 3: Choose Domain Type (Personal vs Company)

This step is critical.

### A. Personal (Individual) Domain

Used when:
- Registering for a personal website
- Portfolio
- Blog
- Personal project

Domain name rules:
- Should match your **name**, **nickname**, or **personal brand**
- Example: `nicksubash.com.np`

---

### B. Company / Organization Domain

Used when:
- Registering a business website
- Company email domain
- Official organization website

Domain name rules:
- Must closely match the **registered company name**
- Example: `sunleafsystem.com.np`

---

## Step 4: Prepare Required Documents

### Documents for Individuals (Personal Domain)

You will need:
- Citizenship certificate (scanned copy)
- Domain request letter (signed)
- Optional: brief explanation of the domain purpose

📌 Note:
- The name on the citizenship must match the account holder name

---

### Documents for Companies / Organizations

You will need:
- Company registration certificate
- PAN or VAT certificate
- Official request letter (on company letterhead)
- Authorized person’s citizenship (in some cases)

📌 Note:
- The domain name must match the **exact or shortened company name**
- Mismatch is the most common reason for rejection

---

## Step 5: Upload Documents and Submit Application

1. Upload all required documents in **clear, readable format**
2. Double-check:
   - File clarity
   - Correct document type
   - Proper naming
3. Submit the application

Once submitted, the status will show as **Pending**.

---

## Step 6: Review & Approval Process

After submission:
- Your application is reviewed manually
- You may receive:
  - Approval
  - Rejection with comments
  - Request for additional clarification

### Typical Approval Time

- ⏱ **1 to 3 working days** in most cases
- Can take longer during:
  - Public holidays
  - High application volume

---

## Common Reasons for Domain Rejection

Be careful about these points:

- Domain name does not match your name or company
- Documents are unclear or incomplete
- Request letter is missing or improperly written
- Account details do not match uploaded documents
- Using unrelated or commercial-looking names without proof
